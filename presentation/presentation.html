Section 2.0 : Engineering Motivation

Problem Statement & Real-World Inefficiencies

Quantified Core Challenges Faced Across Smallholder Agrarian Workflows

Slide 03 / 20
1. Over-Fertilization & Soil Degradation Soil Pathology

Excessive application of chemical urea and DAP without soil chemistry matching destroys beneficial microbiomes and leads to secondary nutrient lockouts:

  • Severe ground leaching of excess Nitrates into rural potable water supplies.
  • Imbalanced N:P:K application ratios (often skewed 8:3:1 versus standard 4:2:1).
  • Annual fertilizer expenditure inflated by 35% without corresponding yield uplift.
2. Groundwater Depletion & Inefficient Flooding Hydrological Crisis

Agricultural irrigation accounts for 89% of national groundwater extraction, predominantly delivered via unmetered surface flooding:

  • Lack of stage-specific volumetric calculation results in severe root suffocation.
  • Absence of delivery method guidance (Drip, Sprinkler, Alternate Wetting & Drying).
  • Estimated 40% of irrigation water lost to non-beneficial deep percolation and evaporation.
3. Middlemen Exploitation at Physical Mandis Market Injustice

Farmers are constrained to physical APMC markets where commission brokers dictate procurement prices:

  • Distress selling caused by perishability and lack of digital listing power.
  • Brokers capture 25% to 35% margin between producer and terminal wholesale buyer.
  • Opaque physical ledgers without verifiable transaction histories.
4. Delayed Pest Diagnostics & Eco-Toxicity Pathogen Control

Inability to rapidly cross-reference symptoms leads to catastrophic late-stage crop blight:

  • Reliance on unscientific chemical cocktails that decimate natural pollinator species.
  • Zero awareness or access to biological and organic bio-control alternatives.
  • Pathogen resistance cycles caused by incorrect active-ingredient dosage per acre.

Pathology lookup indexed on crop for sub-millisecond lookups.

Third Normal Form (3NF) Compliance Justification Database Theory
First Normal Form (1NF): All attributes contain atomic scalar values (no multi-valued repeating groups or arrays).
Second Normal Form (2NF): All non-key attributes are fully functionally dependent on the primary keys. Zero partial key dependencies.
Third Normal Form (3NF): No transitive functional dependencies exist (e.g. non-key columns do not depend on other non-key columns).

Annotated with @Service. Encapsulates algorithms.

@Service public class CalculatorService { public FertilizerResponse compute( FertilizerRequest req) { double soilFactor = getFactor(req.getSoil()); double n = baseN * soilFactor * req.getAcres(); double p = baseP * soilFactor * req.getAcres(); double k = baseK * soilFactor * req.getAcres(); return new FertilizerResponse(n, p, k); } }
